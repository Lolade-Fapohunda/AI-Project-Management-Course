# UAT Plan

## Purpose

The User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Plan defines how business users will validate that the solution meets business needs and is ready for operational use.

UAT focuses on **business acceptance**, not simply whether the technology functions.

## 2. UAT Objectives

* Validate business requirements.
* Confirm expected user workflows.
* Validate business acceptance criteria.
* Identify business-impacting defects.
* Confirm the solution is suitable for intended users.
* Provide evidence for the release decision.

## 5. UAT Acceptance Criteria

UAT should confirm:

* [ ] Required business workflows function as expected.
* [ ] Business requirements are satisfied.
* [ ] Acceptance criteria are met.
* [ ] Appropriate users can complete required tasks.
* [ ] Security and access requirements are satisfied.
* [ ] Significant defects are resolved or formally accepted.
* [ ] Business owner approval is obtained.

## 7. UAT Exit Criteria

UAT is complete when:

* Required scenarios have been executed.
* Critical business defects are resolved.
* High-severity defects are resolved or formally accepted.
* Business acceptance criteria are satisfied.
* Results are documented.
* Business owner approval is obtained.

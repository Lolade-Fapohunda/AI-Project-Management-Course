## Key Discovery Questions

Use stakeholder discussions to understand:

1. What problem are you experiencing?
2. What does the current process look like?
3. What would success look like?
4. What concerns do you have about the proposed solution?
5. What decisions do you need to influence?
6. What information or evidence do you need?
7. What would cause you to reject the solution?

# AI Risk, Security & Governance

## Purpose

This template identifies AI-specific risks, security concerns, governance requirements, controls, accountability, and escalation procedures.

The Project Manager is responsible for ensuring these concerns are identified, assigned, governed, and addressed—not for personally implementing every technical control.

## 3. AI Risk Categories

Consider:

* Hallucination
* Incorrect answers
* Data leakage
* Unauthorized access
* Privacy
* Prompt injection
* Model misuse
* Poor data quality
* Bias
* Inadequate human oversight
* Third-party/vendor risk
* Regulatory or compliance risk
* Availability or performance risk
* Knowledge or model drift

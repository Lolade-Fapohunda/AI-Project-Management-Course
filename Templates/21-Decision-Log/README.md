# Decision Log

## Purpose

The Decision Log records significant project decisions, the evidence behind them, who made them, and their impact.

It creates an auditable history of project judgment and helps prevent repeated debates.

## Decision Escalation

Escalate a decision when:

* It exceeds the Project Manager's authority.
* It materially affects scope, budget, or schedule.
* It introduces significant security or compliance risk.
* Stakeholders cannot reach agreement.
* Executive or governance approval is required.

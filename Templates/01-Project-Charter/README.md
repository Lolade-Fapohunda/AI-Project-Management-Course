# Project Charter

## Purpose

The Project Charter formally authorizes the project and establishes its purpose, objectives, scope, stakeholders, governance, and success measures.

Use this template at project initiation and update it only when major changes require formal approval.

## PM Quality Check

Before approving the Charter, confirm:

* [ ] Business problem is clearly defined.
* [ ] Business objective is measurable.
* [ ] Scope and out-of-scope boundaries are documented.
* [ ] Success measures are measurable.
* [ ] Key stakeholders are identified.
* [ ] Major assumptions and constraints are documented.
* [ ] Initial risks are identified.
* [ ] Governance and decision rights are clear.
* [ ] Sponsor and business owner approval is obtained.

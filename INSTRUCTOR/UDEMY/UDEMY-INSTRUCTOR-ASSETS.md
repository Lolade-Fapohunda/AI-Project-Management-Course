# Udemy Instructor Assets

## Purpose

This document contains the instructor-facing assets for the AI Project Management Course on Udemy.

The assets establish a consistent professional presentation for the course and support the student experience from enrollment through completion.

# Big Promises

By completing this course, you will be able to:

1. **Lead AI projects from business problem to production.**

   You will learn how to manage the full AI project lifecycle from problem definition through requirements, planning, evaluation, governance, release, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

2. **Manage AI projects without becoming an AI engineer.**

   You will develop enough AI knowledge to communicate with technical teams, understand dependencies, challenge unsupported claims, and manage AI-specific project risks.

3. **Make stronger AI Project Management decisions using evidence.**

   You will learn how to evaluate requirements, AI quality, data readiness, security, UAT, release readiness, KPIs, and operational evidence before making project decisions.

4. **Build practical AI Project Management portfolio evidence.**

   You will create and use professional Project Management artifacts throughout the course and bring them together in an end-to-end capstone portfolio.

5. **Know when an AI product is actually ready to move forward.**

   You will practice making **Go, Proceed With Conditions, Hold, and No-Go** decisions based on evidence rather than schedule pressure or technical enthusiasm.

6. **Understand how to manage an AI product after launch.**

   You will learn how to monitor AI quality, business outcomes, risks, incidents, user feedback, and changes and use that evidence to drive continuous improvement.

## Welcome

Welcome to the AI Project Management Course.

Artificial Intelligence is changing how organizations build products, automate processes, and deliver services.

But managing an AI project requires more than understanding the technology.

As a Project Manager, your responsibility is to understand the business problem, align stakeholders, manage requirements and scope, understand technical dependencies, evaluate AI quality, manage risk, coordinate testing, assess release readiness, and monitor the product after deployment.

# Course Introduction Checklist

Before recording the course introduction, confirm that it communicates:

* What the course teaches.
* Who the course is for.
* What students will achieve.
* That the PM Track is required.
* That the Build-Along is optional.
* That students do not need to become AI engineers.
* That students do not need to code, build, or deploy an AI application to complete the core course, capstone, or final assessment.
* That Petadel PolicyAssist AI is the reference project.
* That students may use their own AI product or use case.
* That the course is practical and evidence-based.

# Promotional Messaging Principles

Course marketing should:

* Focus on the skills students will develop.
* Describe practical outcomes.
* Avoid guaranteed career outcomes.
* Avoid guaranteed salary or income claims.
* Avoid unsupported claims about certification.
* Avoid presenting the course as AI engineering training.
* Clearly communicate the optional nature of the technical Build-Along.
* Emphasize Project Management judgment and evidence-based decision-making.

# Instructor Content Standard

All instructor-facing course communication should maintain the course's core principles:

**Business First**

Start with the business problem and desired outcome.

**Evidence Based**

Use measurable evidence rather than assumptions.

**Risk Aware**

Identify and manage AI, security, data, operational, and governance risks.

**User Focused**

Consider whether the solution actually works for its intended users.

**Lifecycle Oriented**

Continue managing the AI product after release.

**Decision Driven**

Connect evidence to decisions and actions.

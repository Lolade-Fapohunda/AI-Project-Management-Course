# Udemy Pricing & Positioning

## Purpose

This document establishes the recommended pricing and market positioning for the AI Project Management Course on Udemy.

The course is positioned as a practical, end-to-end AI Project Management course for professionals who need to lead AI initiatives without becoming AI engineers.

---

# Course Positioning

## Primary Position

**Practical AI Project Management from Business Problem to Production**

The course differentiates itself by combining:

* Project Management discipline
* AI fundamentals
* AI-specific risk and governance
* Requirements and product planning
* AI evaluation and quality
* Testing and UAT
* Release readiness
* Production monitoring
* Continuous improvement
* A complete capstone
* Portfolio-ready Project Management evidence
* An optional hands-on AI Build-Along

The course is not positioned as an AI engineering course, coding bootcamp, or generic course about using ChatGPT for Project Management.

---

# Target Student

The primary audience includes:

* Project Managers
* IT Project Managers
* Technical Project Managers
* Program and Delivery Managers
* Product Managers working with AI products
* Business Analysts supporting AI initiatives
* Technology professionals transitioning into AI Project Management

The course is particularly appropriate for professionals who need to communicate with technical teams and make decisions involving AI requirements, quality, risk, data, governance, testing, and release readiness.

---

# Competitive Positioning

Current Udemy offerings show several established categories around AI and Project Management.

Examples include courses focused on using AI tools to improve traditional Project Management workflows, AI literacy for Project Managers, technical AI Project Management, and PMI-CPMAI certification preparation. Current examples range from approximately 1.25 hours to more than 14 hours and include both broad AI-for-PM courses and certification-focused programs.

This course should occupy a different position:

**AI Project Delivery Leadership**

Rather than primarily teaching students how to use AI to perform Project Management tasks, the course teaches students how to **manage projects that themselves use AI**.

---

# Recommended Price Strategy

## Recommended List Price

**$79.99 USD**

This establishes the course as a substantial professional course rather than a low-cost introductory tutorial.

The course contains 13 modules, practical exercises, 22 reusable Project Management templates, a complete capstone, portfolio development, a final assessment, and an optional technical Build-Along.

---

## Recommended Launch Position

For the initial launch, participate in Udemy's pricing and promotional ecosystem rather than trying to compete solely through a permanently low price.

Udemy's Deals Program can adjust promotional pricing based on market conditions, course performance, conversion data, and other factors. Udemy also maintains localized price tiers by market.

The recommended approach is therefore:

**List Price:** $79.99

**Expected Promotional Position:** Allow Udemy to optimize the promotional price where appropriate.

**Instructor-Created Promotion:** Use selectively for launch and audience-building campaigns.

Do not position the course as a permanently discounted bargain.

---

# Pricing Principles

The course should:

* Remain affordable.
* Communicate professional value.
* Avoid competing solely on price.
* Avoid claiming guaranteed career or income outcomes.
* Allow Udemy's marketplace pricing system to respond to market conditions.
* Be reviewed using actual enrollment and conversion evidence.

Udemy states that promotional prices can vary based on market conditions and course performance, and participation in promotions is not guaranteed.

---

# Revenue Consideration

Udemy does not charge instructors a fee to create and host courses. Paid-course instructors participate in Udemy's revenue-sharing model.

The financial objective should therefore be:

**Accessible Price + Strong Conversion + High Student Value + Positive Reviews + Sustainable Enrollment**

The first objective is not to maximize the list price.

The first objective is to establish a strong course with demonstrable student value.

---

**You do not need to become an AI engineer to lead an AI project.**

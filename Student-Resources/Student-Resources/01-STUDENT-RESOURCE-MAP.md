* Monitoring

# 3. Student Build-Along — Optional

[Open the Student Build-Along](https://github.com/Lolade-Fapohunda/AI-Project-Management-Course/tree/main/Student-Build-Along)

The Build-Along is optional and supports the core Project Management Track.

You may choose:

### Option A — Petadel PolicyAssist

Follow the instructor's fictional Petadel PolicyAssist AI project.

### Option B — Your Own AI Product

Apply the same Project Management process to your own AI product or use case.

The Build-Along provides hands-on experience without requiring you to become an AI engineer.

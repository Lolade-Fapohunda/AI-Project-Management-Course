# Student Start Here

Welcome to the **AI Project Management Course**.

This course is designed to help you understand how to **plan, manage, evaluate, govern, test, release, monitor, and continuously improve AI products** from a Project Management (PM) perspective.

You do **not** need to be an AI engineer or software developer to complete the core course.

---

# Step 1 — Understand the Course Structure

The course has two connected learning paths:

### PM Track — Required

The PM Track is the core course and is required for all students.

You will learn how to manage an AI project across its lifecycle:

**Business Problem → Requirements → Scope → Planning → AI Solution → Data → Evaluation → Risk → Testing → Release → Monitoring → Continuous Improvement**

The PM Track focuses on decision-making, documentation, stakeholder management, governance, quality, risk, and business outcomes.

### Build-Along — Optional

The Build-Along gives you hands-on experience with a working AI product.

You can:

**Option A — Follow the Petadel PolicyAssist reference project**

or

**Option B — Apply the same PM process to your own AI product or use case.**

You do not have to build an AI product to complete the course.

# The Core PM Mindset

Throughout the course, do not focus only on whether the technology works.

A working AI application is **not automatically production-ready**.

As the PM, you must evaluate:

**Business Value + Requirements + Scope + Quality + Security + Governance + Testing + User Acceptance + Performance + Monitoring + Risk + Evidence**

The goal is not to become the person who writes every line of code.

The goal is to become the person who can **lead the project, ask the right questions, challenge assumptions, understand the evidence, coordinate the team, manage risk, and make the right decision at the right time.**

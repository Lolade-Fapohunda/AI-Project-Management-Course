# AI Project Management: Lead AI Projects from Business Problem to Production

## Course Subtitle

Learn to manage AI projects, requirements, risk, evaluation, governance, testing, UAT, release, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

---

# Course Description

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is changing how organizations build products, automate processes, support decisions, and deliver services.

But successful AI projects require more than selecting a model or building an application.

Project Managers must understand the business problem, stakeholders, requirements, scope, data, AI technology, quality, risk, security, governance, testing, release readiness, and production performance.

This practical AI Project Management course teaches you how to manage the complete AI project lifecycle from business problem identification through production and continuous improvement.

You will learn how to:

* Define and validate AI business problems.
* Determine whether AI is the appropriate solution.
* Identify and manage stakeholders.
* Translate business needs into measurable requirements.
* Define scope and manage priorities.
* Build and prioritize AI product backlogs.
* Define a Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
* Understand AI architecture and technical dependencies.
* Evaluate data and knowledge readiness.
* Define AI evaluation criteria and measurable quality thresholds.
* Manage AI-specific risks.
* Address security, privacy, and governance concerns.
* Coordinate testing and User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
* Evaluate pilot results.
* Assess production readiness.
* Develop release and deployment plans.
* Define rollback and hypercare requirements.
* Establish production monitoring.
* Manage incidents and continuous improvement.
* Make evidence-based Go, Proceed With Conditions, Hold, and No-Go decisions.
* Build professional AI Project Management portfolio evidence.

The course is designed for Project Managers and professionals who need to lead AI initiatives without becoming AI engineers.

You do not need to be a programmer, data scientist, machine learning engineer, or AI developer to complete the core course.

You will learn enough about AI technology to communicate with technical teams, understand project dependencies, challenge unsupported technical claims, recognize AI-specific risks, and make informed Project Management decisions.

---

# What You Will Learn

By the end of this course, you should be able to:

1. Frame an AI project around a clearly defined business problem and measurable business outcomes.

2. Analyze stakeholders, competing objectives, user needs, and project constraints.

3. Develop business, functional, non-functional, and AI-specific requirements.

4. Create and prioritize an AI product backlog and define an MVP.

5. Understand AI technologies, architecture, dependencies, and technical risks from a Project Management perspective.

6. Evaluate data quality, authority, versioning, ownership, access, and knowledge governance.

7. Define AI evaluation datasets, metrics, thresholds, and quality criteria.

8. Manage AI risk, security, privacy, and governance.

9. Coordinate functional testing, negative testing, edge-case testing, regression testing, security testing, UAT, and pilots.

10. Evaluate release readiness, deployment strategy, rollback readiness, user readiness, and operational readiness.

11. Establish AI monitoring, KPI management, incident response, and continuous improvement.

12. Lead an end-to-end AI project and communicate evidence-based recommendations to technical and business stakeholders.

13. Produce professional Project Management artifacts and portfolio-ready evidence.

# Course Structure

The course follows the AI Project Management lifecycle:

**Foundations → Problem Discovery → Requirements → Agile Planning → AI Technology → Data & Knowledge → AI Evaluation → Risk & Governance → Testing & UAT → Release → Monitoring → Capstone → Final Assessment**

The course contains 13 modules.

## Module 1 — AI Project Management Foundations

Understand the role of the AI Project Manager, AI-specific project considerations, the project lifecycle, and the difference between technical completion and business success.

## Module 2 — Problem Discovery & Stakeholder Engagement

Learn how to define the real business problem, identify stakeholders, understand competing objectives, determine root causes, and evaluate AI suitability.

## Module 3 — Business Analysis & Requirements

Translate business and user needs into measurable, testable requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and traceability.

## Module 4 — Agile Product Planning

Manage backlogs, priorities, scope, MVP definition, dependencies, releases, Definition of Ready, and Definition of Done.

## Module 5 — AI Technology Fundamentals

Understand Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), embeddings, vector databases, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), grounding, and AI architecture.

## Module 6 — AI Data & Knowledge Management

Evaluate data readiness, data quality, authority, versioning, metadata, ownership, document ingestion, access controls, and knowledge governance.

## Module 7 — AI Evaluation & Quality

Learn how to measure retrieval accuracy, answer accuracy, hallucination, citation correctness, unsupported-question behavior, precision, recall, and other AI quality dimensions.

## Module 8 — AI Risk, Security & Governance

Identify and manage AI-specific risks, security concerns, privacy issues, access control, prompt injection, data leakage, vendor risk, governance, and human oversight.

## Module 9 — Testing, UAT & Pilot

Coordinate testing, defect management, UAT, pilot activities, acceptance criteria, and exit decisions.

## Module 10 — Release & Deployment Management

Assess production readiness, deployment strategy, release criteria, rollback, training, communications, go-live planning, and hypercare.

## Module 11 — Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

Monitor AI quality, performance, security, user satisfaction, business outcomes, incidents, drift, changes, and improvement opportunities.

## Module 12 — Capstone & Portfolio

Bring the complete AI Project Management lifecycle together through an end-to-end capstone and professional portfolio.

## Module 13 — Final Assessment

Demonstrate your AI Project Management knowledge, judgment, risk awareness, and decision-making through scenario-based assessment.

# Capstone Project

The course uses **Petadel PolicyAssist AI** as its reference capstone project.

**Organization:** Petadel Technology Services (PTS)

PolicyAssist is a fictional internal Generative AI policy knowledge assistant designed to help employees locate and understand authoritative company policy information.

The project addresses issues such as:

* Difficult policy discovery
* Multiple documents and versions
* Outdated information
* Unclear authority
* Knowledge governance
* Retrieval quality
* Grounding
* Citation
* Security
* User acceptance
* Release readiness
* Production monitoring

You may use the Petadel PolicyAssist AI reference project or apply the same Project Management framework to your own AI product or use case.

Your capstone should demonstrate:

**Business Problem → Requirements → Scope → AI Solution → Data → Evaluation → Risk → Testing → Release → Monitoring → Continuous Improvement**

The goal is not simply to demonstrate a working application.

The goal is to demonstrate that you can **manage an AI project responsibly and make defensible Project Management decisions using evidence.**

# What Makes This Course Different

This course focuses on the Project Manager's responsibility to connect:

**Business → Users → Requirements → Technology → Data → Quality → Risk → Governance → Delivery → Outcomes**

You will not simply learn AI terminology.

You will practice asking:

**What problem are we solving?**

**What evidence do we have?**

**What could go wrong?**

**What needs to be validated?**

**Who owns the decision?**

**Is the product ready?**

**What should happen next?**

The course emphasizes evidence-based decisions rather than assumptions, technical enthusiasm, or schedule pressure.

---

# Decision-Making Framework

Throughout the course, you will use:

**KPI → Target → Actual → Trend → Threshold → Action**

You will also apply the decision framework:

**Proceed → Proceed With Conditions → Hold → No-Go**

The objective is to develop the ability to make defensible recommendations based on project evidence.

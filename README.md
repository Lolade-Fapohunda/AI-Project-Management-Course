# AI-Project-Management-Course
# AI Project Management Course

## Welcome

This course is designed to train Project Managers to successfully lead projects that use Artificial Intelligence (AI).

The focus is not on becoming an AI engineer.

The focus is on learning how to:

* Identify the right business problem for AI
* Engage stakeholders
* Define clear requirements
* Plan and prioritize AI products
* Understand AI technology at the Project Manager level
* Evaluate data and knowledge readiness
* Define AI quality and evaluation criteria
* Manage AI risk, security, and governance
* Plan testing, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and pilots
* Manage release and deployment
* Monitor AI products after launch
* Lead continuous improvement
* Make evidence-based Go, Hold, or No-Go decisions

---

## Course Organization

The course contains **13 modules** covering the complete AI project lifecycle.

### Module 1: AI Project Management Foundations

Learn the role of the AI Project Manager, how AI projects differ from traditional technology projects, and how to evaluate whether AI is actually the right solution.

### Module 2: Problem Discovery & Stakeholder Engagement

Learn how to identify the real business problem, analyze stakeholders, uncover root causes, and define desired business outcomes.

### Module 3: Business Analysis & Requirements

Learn how to translate business needs into business requirements, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria.

### Module 4: Agile Product Planning

Learn how to build and prioritize product backlogs, define epics and user stories, plan releases, manage dependencies, and control scope.

### Module 5: AI Technology Fundamentals

Learn the AI concepts a Project Manager must understand, including machine learning, generative AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), embeddings, vector databases, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), grounding, and hallucinations.

### Module 6: AI Data & Knowledge Management

Learn how data readiness, ownership, authority, versioning, metadata, document ingestion, data quality, and access control affect AI projects.

### Module 7: AI Evaluation & Quality

Learn how to evaluate AI systems using measurable quality criteria such as retrieval accuracy, answer accuracy, grounding, hallucination rate, citation correctness, and response latency.

### Module 8: AI Risk, Security & Governance

Learn how to identify and manage AI risks, security concerns, privacy issues, access control, prompt injection, data leakage, governance, human oversight, and third-party risk.

### Module 9: Testing, UAT & Pilot

Learn how to plan functional testing, negative testing, edge-case testing, security testing, regression testing, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), defect management, and pilot programs.

### Module 10: Release & Deployment Management

Learn how to determine release readiness, manage deployment dependencies, communicate changes, prepare users, establish rollback procedures, and manage go-live.

### Module 11: Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

Learn how to monitor AI systems after launch, identify drift and performance problems, manage feedback, conduct root-cause analysis, and prioritize improvements.

### Module 12: Capstone & Portfolio

Apply the complete course to the **Petadel PolicyAssist AI** capstone project and produce a professional AI Project Management portfolio.

### Module 13: Final Assessment

Demonstrate your ability to analyze AI project scenarios and make sound Project Management decisions.

The Build Track is not intended to turn students into AI engineers.

---

## Learning Levels

Course activities are categorized by the level of involvement required.

| Level        | Meaning                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **BUILD**    | Implement or directly demonstrate the capability                   |
| **DESIGN**   | Create, define, or review a project artifact or technical approach |
| **SIMULATE** | Make a realistic Project Management decision                       |
| **THEORY**   | Understand the concept without implementing it                     |

This distinction prevents the course from becoming a programming course while still providing meaningful hands-on AI experience.

## Capstone Project

### Petadel PolicyAssist AI

The course capstone is an AI-powered policy assistance solution for **Petadel Technology Services (PTS)**.

The business problem is that employees have difficulty locating and understanding authoritative company policies.

PolicyAssist is designed to retrieve relevant policy information from approved documents and provide grounded responses with appropriate source citations.

The project addresses:

* Policy discovery
* Document ingestion
* Knowledge management
* Semantic retrieval
* AI-generated responses
* Grounding
* Citation
* Access control
* Evaluation
* Security
* Governance
* User Acceptance Testing
* Release management
* Monitoring
* Continuous improvement

The capstone is introduced during the course and fully executed in **Module 12**.

---

## Capstone Lifecycle

The project follows the complete AI project lifecycle:

**Initiation → Discovery → Requirements → Planning → Architecture → Build → Evaluation → Security & Governance → UAT → Release → Monitoring → Continuous Improvement**

This lifecycle is used to connect the concepts taught throughout the course.

---

## Course Philosophy

The course is built around one principle:

> **An AI Project Manager does not need to build every technical component. They need to understand enough to lead the people, decisions, risks, requirements, controls, and outcomes surrounding those components.**

The goal is not simply to produce a working AI application.

The goal is to demonstrate the ability to **lead an AI project from business problem through production and continuous improvement.**

# the organization
## Code of Conduct Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes standards of professional and ethical conduct for individuals working on behalf of the organization.

## 2. Professional Conduct

Employees and contractors are expected to conduct themselves professionally and respectfully when interacting with coworkers, customers, partners, and other individuals.

Employees must perform their responsibilities honestly, responsibly, and in good faith.

## 3. Respectful Workplace

The organization is committed to maintaining a workplace where individuals are treated with dignity and respect.

Harassment, bullying, intimidation, threats, and other inappropriate conduct are not permitted.

Employees should raise concerns about inappropriate workplace behavior through the appropriate reporting channels.

## 4. Discrimination and Harassment

The organization does not tolerate unlawful discrimination or harassment in the workplace.

Employees must not engage in inappropriate conduct based on characteristics protected by applicable law.

Concerns regarding discrimination or harassment should be reported to Human Resources or through an approved reporting channel.

## 5. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations in which personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the organization.

Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest to their manager or Human Resources.

Employees must not use their position at the organization for unauthorized personal gain.

## 6. Confidential Information

Employees must protect confidential organization information and must not disclose confidential information to unauthorized individuals.

Confidentiality obligations continue after an individual's employment or engagement with the organization ends when applicable.

## 7. Company Property and Resources

organization property, equipment, systems, and resources must be used responsibly and for authorized purposes.

Employees must not intentionally misuse, damage, or remove company property without authorization.

## 8. Gifts and Business Courtesies

Employees must not accept gifts, payments, or other benefits that could improperly influence business decisions.

Employees should consult their manager or Human Resources when they are uncertain whether a gift or business courtesy is appropriate.

## 9. Accurate Records

Employees are expected to provide accurate information in company records, reports, expense submissions, timekeeping systems, and other official documentation.

Employees must not knowingly falsify or misrepresent company records.

## 10. Compliance with Laws and Policies

Employees and contractors must comply with applicable laws, regulations, and the organization policies while performing work on behalf of the company.

Employees are responsible for understanding and following policies applicable to their responsibilities.

## 11. Reporting Concerns

Employees are encouraged to report suspected misconduct, policy violations, or unethical behavior through appropriate the organization reporting channels.

Reports should be made in good faith.

the organization prohibits retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith or participate in an authorized investigation.

## 12. Investigations

The organization may investigate reported concerns or suspected violations of company policies.

Employees are expected to cooperate honestly with authorized investigations.

## 13. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in corrective or disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or engagement, when appropriate.

## 14. Questions

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to Human Resources.

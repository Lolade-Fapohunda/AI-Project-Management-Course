# Milestone 9 — Testing & User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

## PM Objective

Validate that the AI product meets business requirements and is ready for real users.

## Hands-On Objective

Execute defined test scenarios and evaluate whether the product is ready to move toward release.

## PM Activities

Evaluate:

- Functional requirements
- Acceptance criteria
- AI quality
- Security controls
- User experience
- Error handling
- Unsupported questions
- Citation behavior
- Performance
- Business readiness

## Hands-On Activity

1. Select representative business scenarios.
2. Define the expected outcome for each scenario.
3. Execute the tests.
4. Record Pass/Fail results.
5. Document defects or gaps.
6. Assign severity.
7. Determine whether issues must be resolved before release.
8. Complete UAT scenarios from the business-user perspective.

## Deliverable

Complete a UAT results record containing:

- Scenario
- Expected result
- Actual result
- Pass/Fail
- Severity
- Business impact
- Recommended action

## Release Recommendation

Based on testing and UAT, recommend:

**Go → Hold → No-Go**

Document the evidence supporting your recommendation.

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to answer:

**Does the product meet the requirements and business expectations well enough for release?**

# Milestone 5 — AI Architecture

## PM Objective

Understand the major components of an AI solution and the PM decisions, dependencies, risks, and trade-offs associated with them.

## Hands-On Objective

Trace how a user request moves through the AI product from input to response.

## PM Activities

Understand the role of:

- User Interface (UI)
- Policy documents
- Document processing
- Embeddings
- Vector database
- Retrieval
- Large Language Model (LLM)
- Grounding
- Citations

## Hands-On Activity

Using the reference application or your own AI project:

1. Identify the major components.
2. Identify what each component is responsible for.
3. Identify dependencies between components.
4. Identify at least three technical risks that could affect the project.
5. Identify one quality risk associated with AI-generated responses.
6. Identify one decision the PM must make about the solution.

## PM Deliverable

Create a simple AI solution architecture diagram or architecture summary.

For each major component, document:

- Purpose
- PM concern
- Dependency
- Risk

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to explain:

**How does the AI solution work at a high level, and what does the PM need to manage at each stage?**

> You are not expected to engineer these components. The objective is to understand them well enough to manage the product, requirements, risks, quality, and delivery.

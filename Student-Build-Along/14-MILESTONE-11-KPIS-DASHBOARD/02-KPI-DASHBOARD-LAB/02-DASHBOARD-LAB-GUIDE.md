# KPI Dashboard Lab

## Objective

Build and interpret a simple AI product KPI dashboard.

This is an **optional technical Build-Along**.

All students complete the PM analysis in Milestone 11.

## What You Will Build

You will progressively add:

1. KPI data
2. KPI cards
3. Target vs. Actual visualization
4. Trend visualization
5. Status and threshold logic
6. PM decision support

## Guided Build Method

Follow this sequence:

**Watch → Build → Run → Test → Interpret → Document → Decide**

You will modify the provided starter code rather than building the application from scratch.

---

## Step 1 — Review the KPI Data

Open:

`01-DASHBOARD-STARTER.py`

Review the KPI names, targets, and actual values.

Ask:

* What does each KPI measure?
* Is higher or lower performance better?
* Which KPIs are currently meeting target?
* Which KPIs require attention?

### PM Decision

Identify the first KPI you would investigate and explain why.

---

## Step 2 — Review the KPI Cards

Run the application and review the KPI cards.

The cards provide a quick view of current product performance.

### PM Question

If you had only 30 seconds to assess product health, what would the KPI cards tell you?

---

## Step 3 — Add Target vs. Actual

Add a visual comparison showing each KPI's target and actual performance.

### PM Question

Which KPIs are above target and which are below target?

---

## Step 4 — Add a Trend

Add performance-over-time data and a trend visualization.

### PM Question

Is the product improving, declining, or remaining stable?

---

## Step 5 — Add Status & Threshold Logic

Add logic that identifies each KPI as:

* On Target
* Needs Attention
* Below Threshold

### PM Question

Which KPI requires immediate PM attention?

---

## Step 6 — Test the Dashboard

Run the dashboard and verify:

* KPI cards display correctly.
* Target and actual values are correct.
* The trend is visible.
* Status logic works.
* Threshold conditions are identified correctly.

Document any issues you find.

---

## Step 7 — Interpret the Results

Review the completed dashboard.

Identify:

* KPIs meeting target
* KPIs missing target
* Improving trends
* Declining trends
* Threshold breaches
* Business or product risks

---

## Step 8 — Make the PM Decision

Use the evidence from the dashboard to choose one:

**Continue → Improve → Escalate → Reassess**

Document:

* The evidence
* The business impact
* The recommended action
* The owner
* The next review point

---

## Final PM Question

> What is the product telling us through its KPIs, and what should the PM do about it?

## Important

The goal is not to become a software engineer.

The goal is to understand how technology can provide evidence that supports effective PM decisions.

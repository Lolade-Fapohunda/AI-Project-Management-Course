# Milestone 4 — Backlog & MVP

## PM Objective

Prioritize AI product capabilities based on business value, risk, feasibility, and stakeholder needs.

## Hands-On Objective

Translate requirements into prioritized product capabilities.

## PM Activities

- Convert requirements into user stories
- Define acceptance criteria
- Prioritize the backlog
- Identify the Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
- Identify dependencies
- Identify high-risk capabilities
- Define what is intentionally out of scope

## Hands-On Activity

1. Select at least five requirements.
2. Convert them into user stories.
3. Add acceptance criteria.
4. Prioritize each item.
5. Identify which capabilities belong in the MVP.
6. Identify at least two items that should not be included in the MVP.
7. Document the reasoning behind your prioritization.

## Deliverable

Create or update your product backlog.

Your backlog should clearly show:

- User story
- Business value
- Priority
- Acceptance criteria
- Dependencies
- MVP status

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to answer:

**Why are these capabilities in the MVP, and why were other capabilities deferred?**

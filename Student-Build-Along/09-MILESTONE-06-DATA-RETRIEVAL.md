# Milestone 6 — Data & Retrieval

## PM Objective

Understand how the quality, organization, and authority of AI data affect product performance.

## Hands-On Objective

Observe how the AI product finds relevant information and determine whether the retrieved information is appropriate for the user's question.

## PM Activities

Evaluate:

- Data quality
- Data completeness
- Document authority
- Document version
- Metadata
- Relevance
- Retrieval accuracy
- Outdated or conflicting information
- Data access and permissions

## Hands-On Activity

Using the reference application or your own AI project:

1. Identify the information sources used by the AI product.
2. Identify which sources are considered authoritative.
3. Test several user questions.
4. Review the information retrieved for each question.
5. Determine whether the retrieved information is relevant.
6. Identify at least two retrieval or data-quality risks.
7. Document one improvement recommendation.

## Deliverable

Complete a data and retrieval assessment.

Document:

- Source
- Authority
- Relevance
- Version/status
- Retrieval result
- Issue identified
- Recommended action

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to answer:

**How can poor data, outdated information, or incorrect retrieval affect the AI product—and what can the PM do about it?**

# Build-Along Milestones

The Build-Along is optional and supports the PM Track.

The PM Track is the core course. Students do not need to complete the Build-Along to complete the course, capstone, or Final Assessment.

| Milestone | Course Module | PM Focus               | Hands-On Focus                                   |
| --------- | ------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| 1         | Module 1      | AI project foundation  | Run the starter application                      |
| 2         | Module 2      | Problem & stakeholders | Connect stakeholder needs to the product         |
| 3         | Module 3      | Requirements           | Translate needs into AI requirements             |
| 4         | Module 4      | Backlog & MVP          | Prioritize capabilities                          |
| 5         | Module 5      | AI architecture        | Understand the AI solution from a PM perspective |
| 6         | Module 6      | Data & retrieval       | Explore policy ingestion and retrieval           |
| 7         | Module 7      | AI evaluation          | Evaluate AI quality and performance              |
| 8         | Module 8      | Security & governance  | Apply security and governance controls           |
| 9         | Module 9      | Testing & UAT          | Execute tests and UAT scenarios                  |
| 10        | Module 10     | Release & deployment   | Apply release-readiness decisions                |
| 11        | Module 11     | KPIs & dashboard       | Analyze product performance and KPIs             |
| 12        | Module 12     | Capstone & portfolio   | Package the PM evidence                          |

## Milestone Outcomes

| Milestone                     | Hands-On Outcome                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Foundation**             | You can launch the PolicyAssist starter application, understand its purpose, and identify the major PM-relevant components of the AI product.                             |
| **2. Problem & Stakeholders** | You connect the business problem and stakeholder needs to specific product capabilities and document the resulting priorities.                                            |
| **3. Requirements**           | You turn stakeholder needs into clear AI product requirements and trace each requirement to an expected business or user outcome.                                         |
| **4. Backlog & MVP**          | You create and prioritize an AI product backlog, define the Minimum Viable Product (MVP), and explain what is intentionally included or excluded.                         |
| **5. AI Architecture**        | You can explain the end-to-end AI solution flow and make PM-level decisions about components, dependencies, risks, and trade-offs.                                        |
| **6. Data & Retrieval**       | You can inspect the policy knowledge source, understand how documents become searchable knowledge, and verify that retrieved information supports the product's answers.  |
| **7. AI Evaluation**          | You run or review AI evaluation results, compare performance against defined targets, identify quality gaps, and recommend corrective action.                             |
| **8. Security & Governance**  | You apply security and governance controls to the AI product and make a readiness decision based on identified risks and controls.                                        |
| **9. Testing & UAT**          | You execute defined test and User Acceptance Testing (UAT) scenarios, document results and defects, assess business impact, and make a Go, Hold, or No-Go recommendation. |
| **10. Release & Deployment**  | You assess release readiness across testing, security, UAT, dependencies, rollback, support, and business approval, then make a release decision.                         |
| **11. KPIs & Dashboard**      | You build or analyze the KPI dashboard, compare actual performance against targets and thresholds, identify issues, and make a data-driven PM decision.                   |
| **12. Capstone & Portfolio**  | You integrate your PM evidence into a complete AI project story and produce a portfolio-ready package demonstrating your decisions and outcomes.                          |

## Module 13 Final Assessment

The Final Assessment is part of the required PM Track.

It is **not** a separate Build-Along milestone.

All students complete the Final Assessment regardless of whether they participate in the optional Build-Along.

The Final Assessment independently validates end-to-end AI Project Management judgment through knowledge questions, scenarios, KPI interpretation, release decisions, executive communication, and a final PM challenge.

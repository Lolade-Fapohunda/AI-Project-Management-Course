# Student Build-Along — START HERE

## Welcome

This optional Build-Along gives you hands-on experience applying AI Project Management concepts to an AI product.

You do not need to be an engineer to complete this course.

The Build-Along is designed to help you understand how PM decisions become real AI product capabilities.

---

## Two Ways to Participate

### Option 1 — Follow the Reference Project

Follow the instructor's PolicyAssist AI example throughout the Build-Along.

This gives you a complete example of an AI product moving through the project lifecycle.

### Option 2 — Apply the Process to Your Own AI Project

You may use your own AI product or business use case instead.

Apply the PM decisions, requirements, evaluation, governance, testing, UAT, release, KPI, and monitoring activities to your own project.

You do not need to recreate the instructor's technical application.

## What You Do NOT Need

You do not need:

- Advanced programming skills
- Machine-learning engineering experience
- Data-science experience
- Model-training experience
- Advanced cloud engineering skills

The course is focused on **AI Project Management**.

---

## How the Build-Along Works

Each milestone follows this pattern:

**Learn → Make a PM Decision → Complete the Hands-On Activity → Test → Document → Move Forward**

You will connect technical concepts to PM responsibilities such as:

- Requirements
- Scope
- Prioritization
- Acceptance criteria
- Risk management
- AI evaluation
- Security
- Governance
- Testing
- UAT
- Release readiness
- Deployment
- KPIs
- Monitoring
- Continuous improvement

---

## PM Track vs. Build-Along

### PM Track

The PM Track is the core course and is available to everyone.

You will learn how to manage an AI product throughout its lifecycle.

### Build-Along

The Build-Along is optional.

It provides additional hands-on experience with an AI application.

You can complete the PM Track without completing the Build-Along.

---

## KPI & Dashboard

KPI and dashboard work is part of the core PM Track.

You do NOT need to participate in the Build-Along to complete the KPI and dashboard activities.

All students will learn how to:

1. Define KPIs
2. Establish targets
3. Review actual performance
4. Analyze trends
5. Establish thresholds
6. Interpret results
7. Recommend corrective actions
8. Communicate performance to stakeholders

Build-Along participants may also implement the dashboard technically.

---

## Your Goal

By the end of the course, you should be able to explain and demonstrate how an AI product moves from:

**Business Problem → Requirements → Planning → Build → Evaluation → Governance → Testing → UAT → Release → Monitoring → Continuous Improvement**

The goal is not to become an AI engineer.

The goal is to become an **AI Project Manager who understands enough about the technology to manage the product effectively.**

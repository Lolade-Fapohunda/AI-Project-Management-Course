# Milestone 8 — Security & Governance

## PM Objective

Ensure the AI product operates within defined security, access, governance, and responsible-AI requirements.

## Hands-On Objective

Evaluate whether users can access appropriate information and whether the AI responds safely when evidence or authorization is insufficient.

## PM Activities

Evaluate:

- User access and authorization
- Data protection
- Policy authority
- Version control
- Privacy
- AI governance
- Unsupported questions
- Escalation requirements
- Auditability

## Hands-On Activity

Using the reference application or your own AI project:

1. Identify the different types of users who may access the product.
2. Identify what information each user should be allowed to access.
3. Test an authorized request.
4. Test an unauthorized or restricted request.
5. Test a question where sufficient evidence is unavailable.
6. Determine whether the AI appropriately refuses or escalates.
7. Identify security or governance gaps.
8. Document recommended controls.

## Deliverable

Create a security and governance assessment documenting:

- Risk
- Impact
- Existing control
- Gap
- Recommended control
- Owner
- Priority

## PM Decision

Determine whether the product is:

**Ready → Ready with Conditions → Not Ready**

Document the evidence supporting your decision.

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to answer:

**Can the product provide useful AI assistance while protecting information, enforcing appropriate access, and preventing unsupported guidance?**

# Milestone 2 — Problem & Stakeholders

## PM Objective

Translate the business problem into clear stakeholder needs and understand how different stakeholders define success.

## Hands-On Objective

Use the reference AI product or your own AI project to connect stakeholder needs to product outcomes.

## PM Activities

- Identify primary and secondary stakeholders
- Define stakeholder needs
- Identify pain points
- Identify stakeholder concerns
- Define desired outcomes
- Identify potential conflicts
- Determine how stakeholder input affects scope and priorities

## Hands-On Activity

1. Identify at least five stakeholders.
2. Document each stakeholder's primary need.
3. Identify one concern or risk for each stakeholder.
4. Identify what success looks like for each stakeholder.
5. Identify any conflicting stakeholder priorities.
6. Determine which needs should influence the initial product scope.

## Deliverable

Complete a stakeholder analysis and document at least three PM decisions.

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to answer:

**Who are the stakeholders, what do they need from the AI product, and how should their needs influence product decisions?**

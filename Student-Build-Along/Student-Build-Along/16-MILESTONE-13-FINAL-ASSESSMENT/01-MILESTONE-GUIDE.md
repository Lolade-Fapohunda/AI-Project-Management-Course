# Milestone 13 — Final Assessment Guide

## Objective

This milestone is the final validation of your ability to apply the **Artificial Intelligence (AI) Project Management** lifecycle from project initiation through release, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

You will demonstrate that you can make evidence-based Project Management (PM) decisions across the complete AI product lifecycle.

This is the final milestone of the Student Build-Along.

---

# Assessment Approach

The Final Assessment evaluates your ability to:

* Understand the role of an AI Project Manager.
* Define and protect the business problem.
* Identify and manage stakeholders.
* Translate business needs into requirements.
* Define scope and Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
* Plan and prioritize AI product work.
* Understand AI technology well enough to make informed PM decisions.
* Manage data and knowledge risks.
* Evaluate AI quality and performance.
* Manage AI risk, security, and governance.
* Plan and evaluate testing and User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
* Assess release readiness.
* Make Go, Conditional Go, Hold, or No-Go recommendations.
* Interpret Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
* Use evidence to drive corrective action.
* Communicate recommendations to technical and business stakeholders.
* Manage continuous improvement after release.

You are being assessed on **Project Management judgment**, not your ability to write production-level code.

---

# What You Should Demonstrate

By completing this milestone, you should be able to explain not only **what** should happen during an AI project, but **why**, **when**, and **what decision should be made based on the evidence available**.

You should demonstrate that you can connect:

**Business Problem → Requirements → Scope → AI Solution → Data → Evaluation → Risk → Testing → Release → Monitoring → Improvement**

You should also be able to identify when evidence is insufficient and recommend additional validation rather than making an unsupported decision.

---

# Final Assessment Standard

Your answers should demonstrate:

### 1. Business Alignment

You can connect AI product decisions to the original business problem and intended business outcome.

### 2. Evidence-Based Decision Making

You use measurable evidence, requirements, test results, risks, KPIs, stakeholder feedback, or other documented information to support your decisions.

### 3. AI Product Understanding

You understand the major AI concepts necessary to manage an AI product, including:

* Large Language Model (LLM)
* Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
* Embeddings
* Vector databases
* AI evaluation
* Hallucination
* Data quality
* Data governance
* AI security
* AI monitoring

You are not expected to build these technologies from scratch.

### 4. Lifecycle Management

You understand that an AI project does not end when the product is deployed.

The lifecycle continues through:

**Release → Monitoring → Evaluation → Corrective Action → Continuous Improvement**

### 5. Governance and Risk Awareness

You recognize that AI products require appropriate controls for:

* Security
* Privacy
* Access
* Data quality
* Source authority
* Compliance
* Model behavior
* Human escalation
* Auditability

### 6. Product and Project Judgment

You can distinguish between:

* A project issue and a product issue.
* A technical problem and a business problem.
* A risk and an active issue.
* A performance gap and a threshold breach.
* A defect and a change request.
* A release blocker and a non-blocking issue.

---

# Final PM Checkpoint

Before submitting your assessment, ask yourself:

> **Can I explain the evidence, make the appropriate PM decision, identify the action required, and explain the expected business or product outcome?**

For major decisions, use:

**Evidence → Risk/Impact → Decision → Action → Owner → Outcome**

You have completed Milestone 13 when you have:

* Completed the Final Assessment.

* Completed the release decision exercise.
* Interpreted the KPI scenario.
* Provided an executive-level recommendation.
* Completed the final PM challenge.

---

# Final Course Validation

The purpose of this milestone is not simply to test whether you remember terminology.

You should demonstrate that you can **apply the concepts**.

A strong answer should explain:

**What should happen?**

**Why should it happen?**

**What evidence supports the decision?**

**What risk or impact exists?**

**What should the PM do next?**

---

# Final Principle

An effective AI Project Manager does not manage AI based on assumptions.

You manage the product through evidence, decisions, accountability, and outcomes.

**Problem → Evidence → Decision → Action → Outcome**

This is the standard you should carry forward into real AI projects.

# Module 13: Final Assessment

## Purpose

The Final Assessment measures your ability to independently apply the principles of Artificial Intelligence (AI) Project Management to realistic project situations.

This assessment tests your:

* Project Management (PM) judgment.
* Decision-making.
* Risk management.
* Stakeholder management.
* Requirements and scope management.
* AI project knowledge.
* AI evaluation and quality management.
* Security and governance awareness.
* Testing, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and release readiness.

This assessment is **not** designed to test your ability to memorize the Petadel PolicyAssist AI project.

You should be able to apply the concepts learned throughout this course to other AI products, projects, organizations, and business environments.

---

# Learning Objectives

By completing this assessment, you should be able to:

* Evaluate an AI project from a PM perspective.
* Identify business and technical risks.
* Challenge unsupported technical claims.
* Evaluate requirements and project scope.
* Assess AI data and knowledge readiness.
* Interpret AI evaluation results.
* Manage security and governance concerns.
* Evaluate UAT and release readiness.
* Make evidence-based project decisions.
* Communicate recommendations to technical and business stakeholders.

---

# Assessment Structure

The assessment consists primarily of scenario-based questions.

Questions may require you to:

* Select the best PM decision.
* Explain your reasoning.
* Identify missing evidence.
* Identify project risks.
* Identify affected stakeholders.
* Recommend stakeholder actions.
* Evaluate tradeoffs.
* Determine whether a project should proceed.
* Define conditions that must be satisfied before release.

The strongest answers will explain **why** a decision is appropriate, not simply identify the decision.

---

# Assessment Principles

For every scenario, consider:

## 1. Business Objective

What is the project actually trying to accomplish?

## 2. Evidence

What information supports the decision?

What evidence is missing?

## 3. Risk

What could go wrong?

What would be the business or product impact?

## 4. Stakeholders

Who is affected by the decision?

Who has decision authority?

## 5. Requirements

What must be true for the product to be successful?

## 6. Governance

Who has the authority to approve, reject, or accept the risk?

## 7. Decision

Should the project:

* Proceed.
* Proceed With Conditions.
* Hold.
* Stop.

# Scenario 1: The AI Solution Is Popular

Leadership wants an AI chatbot because competitors are using similar technology.

The business has not clearly identified the problem the chatbot is expected to solve.

### Question

What should the AI Project Manager do first?

A. Select an AI model.

B. Begin development of the chatbot.

C. Define and validate the business problem and desired outcomes.

D. Purchase an AI platform.

# Scenario 2: Conflicting Policy Documents

An AI knowledge system retrieves two versions of the same policy.

One is marked **Active**.

The other has a newer effective date but has not been approved.

### Question

What should the PM recommend?

A. Use the newest document.

B. Use whichever document the AI ranks highest.

C. Use both documents.

D. Hold the conflicting information for authority resolution.

Explain your reasoning.

# Scenario 3: The Model Performs Well

An AI system achieves:

* 94% retrieval accuracy.
* 93% answer accuracy.
* 1% hallucination rate.
* 100% citation correctness.

However, UAT users report that the system frequently refuses questions because they use terminology that differs from the evaluation dataset.

### Question

Should you immediately approve production release?

Explain your reasoning.

Identify the actions you would take before making the final release decision.

# Scenario 4: Deadline Pressure

An AI product is scheduled to launch Monday.

The following items remain incomplete:

* Security testing.
* UAT.
* Final AI evaluation.
* Production monitoring.
* Rollback validation.

Leadership says:

> "We can finish those after launch."

### Question

What should you recommend?

Explain the risks and identify the activities that should be completed before release.

# Scenario 5: The Requirement Is Too Vague

A stakeholder provides the requirement:

> "The AI should be fast and accurate."

### Question

What should you do?

Explain how you would convert this statement into measurable requirements.

Your answer should identify:

* The metric.
* Measurement method.
* Target.
* Threshold.
* Acceptance criteria.
* Test method.

---

# Scenario 6: The Vendor Makes a Claim

A vendor says:

> "Our model has 99% accuracy."

The vendor does not explain:

* Dataset.
* Evaluation methodology.
* Use case.
* Test conditions.
* Definition of accuracy.

### Question

What should you do?

Identify the evidence you would request before accepting the claim.

# Scenario 7: Scope Expansion

During development, leadership requests five additional AI capabilities.

The team is already behind schedule.

### Question

What should you do?

Explain how you would evaluate the requested changes and determine whether they belong in the current release.

* Business value.
* Strategic importance.
* Priority.
* Resources.
* Schedule.
* Dependencies.
* Risk.
* Technical complexity.
* MVP impact.
* Existing commitments.

# Scenario 8: Security Risk

Testing identifies a possibility that users could retrieve information they are not authorized to access.

The development team says:

### Question

What should you do?

# Final Assessment Exercise

## Scenario

You are assigned to manage an AI system for a large organization.

The project is six weeks from its planned launch.

Current status:

* Business problem defined.
* Stakeholders identified.
* Requirements approved.
* MVP backlog prioritized.
* Architecture selected.
* Data ingestion underway.
* Initial evaluation results available.
* Security review underway.
* UAT has not started.
* Monitoring plan is incomplete.
* Rollback has not been tested.
* Leadership wants to maintain the original launch date.

During evaluation, the team discovers that the AI performs well on common questions but performs poorly on several important edge cases.

The development team believes the issues can be corrected after launch.

## Your Task

Prepare a PM recommendation addressing:

1. Should the project remain on schedule?
2. What evidence is missing?
3. What risks exist?
4. Which stakeholders need to be involved?
5. What activities must occur before release?
6. What should happen to the edge cases?
7. What conditions should be required for Go-Live?
8. What is your final recommendation?

Your answer should demonstrate:

**Problem → Evidence → Risk → Stakeholders → Options → Decision → Actions**

The strongest AI Project Managers do not simply ask:

> "Can we launch?"

> **"Do we have sufficient evidence that we should launch?"**

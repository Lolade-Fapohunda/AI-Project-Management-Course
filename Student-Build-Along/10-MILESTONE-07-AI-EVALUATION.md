# Milestone 7 — AI Evaluation

## PM Objective

Determine whether the AI product is performing well enough to meet its defined quality and business requirements.

## Hands-On Objective

Test AI responses against defined scenarios and evaluate the results using measurable criteria.

## Important: You Are Evaluating Quality, Not Just Functionality

A working response is not automatically a good response.

As an AI Project Manager, your job is not simply to confirm that the application runs or produces an answer. Your job is to determine whether the AI is performing **well enough** to support its intended use.

Your evaluation should therefore look for **evidence**, not just successful demonstrations.

Ask:

* Did the AI retrieve the right source?
* Is the answer factually correct?
* Is the answer supported by the retrieved evidence?
* Is the citation correct?
* Does the AI appropriately refuse questions it cannot answer?
* Does performance meet the defined quality target?
* Are there quality gaps that require corrective action?

**The goal is not to prove that the AI works. The goal is to determine, using evidence, whether it works well enough to move forward.**

This distinction is important throughout the Build-Along. A product may function technically while still producing inaccurate, weakly grounded, poorly supported, or otherwise unacceptable results.

## PM Activities

Evaluate:

* Answer accuracy
* Retrieval accuracy
* Hallucination rate
* Citation correctness
* Unsupported-question refusal
* Response latency
* User satisfaction

## Hands-On Activity

1. Select at least five representative user questions.
2. Define the expected result for each question.
3. Run each question through the AI product.
4. Compare the response with the expected result.
5. Check whether supporting evidence was retrieved.
6. Check whether citations are correct.
7. Identify unsupported or incorrect claims.
8. Record the results.
9. Determine whether the product meets the defined quality targets.

## Deliverable

Complete an **AI Evaluation Results Table** containing:

* Test question
* Expected result
* Actual result
* Pass/Fail
* Metric evaluated
* Issue identified
* Recommended action

Your evaluation should document the evidence behind each Pass/Fail determination rather than relying on whether the response simply appears reasonable.

## PM Decision

Based on the evaluation results, determine whether the product should:

**Proceed → Hold → Return for Improvement**

Document the evidence supporting your decision.

A **Proceed** decision does not mean the AI is perfect. It means the available evidence supports moving forward relative to the defined requirements and identified risks.

A **Hold** or **Return for Improvement** decision should identify the quality gap, its impact, and the corrective action needed.

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to answer:

**Is the AI performing well enough to move forward, and what evidence supports that decision?**

Your answer should be based on documented evaluation results, defined criteria, and measurable evidence rather than simply confirming that the application works.

# Milestone 10 — Release & Deployment

## PM Objective

Determine whether the AI product is ready for production and manage the transition from tested product to live service.

## Hands-On Objective

Understand the deployment process and apply release-readiness criteria.

## PM Activities

Evaluate:

- Release readiness
- Open defects
- Security status
- UAT results
- Performance results
- Business approval
- Deployment dependencies
- Rollback readiness
- Support readiness
- Go / Hold / No-Go criteria

## Hands-On Activity

1. Review the results from testing and UAT.
2. Review unresolved defects and risks.
3. Confirm required acceptance criteria are met.
4. Confirm security and governance requirements are addressed.
5. Confirm rollback procedures are defined.
6. Determine whether the product is ready for deployment.
7. If participating in the technical Build-Along, follow the deployment instructions for the application.
8. Document the release decision.

## Deliverable

Create a release-readiness assessment containing:

- Readiness criterion
- Status
- Evidence
- Outstanding issue
- Owner
- Required action

## PM Decision

Make one of the following decisions:

**GO** — Release can proceed.

**HOLD** — Release requires additional work before proceeding.

**NO-GO** — Release should not proceed because critical conditions are not met.

Document the evidence supporting your decision.

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to answer:

**Is the product safe, tested, accepted, supportable, and ready to release—and what is our rollback plan if something goes wrong?**

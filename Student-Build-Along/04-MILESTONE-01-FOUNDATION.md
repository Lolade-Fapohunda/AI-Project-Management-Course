# Milestone 1 — AI Project Foundation

## PM Objective

Understand the AI product, its business problem, project objective, scope, stakeholders, and success measures.

## Hands-On Objective

Run the starter application and connect what you see to the PM decisions made during Module 1.

## PM Activities

- Identify the business problem
- Define the desired business outcome
- Identify key stakeholders
- Define initial scope
- Identify major assumptions
- Identify initial risks
- Define measurable success criteria

## Hands-On Activity

1. Open the student starter application.
2. Run the application.
3. Review the application purpose and user experience.
4. Identify what the application currently does.
5. Identify what it does not yet do.
6. Record at least three PM decisions.

## Deliverable

Complete the PM Decision Log.

Document:

- Decision
- Business reason
- Evidence
- Risk or trade-off
- Expected outcome

## PM Checkpoint

Before moving forward, you should be able to answer:

**What problem are we solving, who are we solving it for, and how will we know the AI product is successful?**
